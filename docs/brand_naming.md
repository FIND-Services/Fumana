# Fumana brand naming standard

Telos is the intelligence brand. Use it wherever the platform produces intelligence.
The CCM is the internal technical architecture. Never write it in the interface.
The one exception: a plain-language description on the responsible-AI screen and ethics page only.
MOE agents are named at point of use: Telos Ingestion Agent, Telos Compliance Agent, Telos Economics Agent, Telos Infrastructure Agent.
Zuri is the human-facing agent. She surfaces what the MOE agents compute. She is never called an MOE agent.
